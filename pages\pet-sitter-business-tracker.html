WHAT IT DOES

Useful totals, with the details still visible.

Enter each visit once. Amount due and remaining balance calculate from your booking inputs. Change the month on the dashboard to review completed visits, payments, outstanding amounts, and logged costs.

The workbook does not synchronize with a calendar, send reminders, process payments, create contracts, or prepare tax returns. Keep sensitive home-access information in a secure system designed for it.

PRODUCT DETAILS

Designed for a solo pet-care workflow

Pet sitting and dog walking involve recurring visits, changing schedules, different rates, and several pets in one household. This tracker is designed to give one person a clear place to record those transactions without setting up a full client-management system.

What is included

  • One Excel workbook in .xlsx format with eight worksheets.
  • Booking and payment tracking with formulas for amount due and balance.
  • Client, pet, service, and expense logs that can be edited for your own workflow.
  • A monthly dashboard and an adjustable visit-rate planning calculator.
  • A Start Here sheet with setup notes, field definitions, and Google Sheets import steps.

Compatibility and privacy

The workbook is built for Microsoft Excel and may be imported into Google Sheets. Spreadsheet formatting can change when another app imports a file, so check the formulas and layout in your chosen app before using it for business records.

Use client and pet identifiers where practical. Do not put door codes, alarm codes, passwords, payment-card data, or sensitive medical records in this workbook. It is a local file, not an encrypted client-record system.

Scope: This is an organizer and planning tool. It does not replace booking software, accounting software, tax advice, legal forms, insurance, or your own business records.

Who it may suit

It may suit a new or solo pet sitter, dog walker, or small pet-care operator who prefers a spreadsheet and wants a straightforward booking and payment log. A business with multiple staff, client portals, recurring scheduling, or automated reminders will likely need dedicated software.
