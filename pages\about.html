THE IDEA

Small tools for the work behind pet care.

SitKit is a pre-launch concept for clear, editable business tools for independent pet sitters and dog walkers.

Start with one useful workbook

The first product is a spreadsheet to organize visits, client and pet records, payments, and expenses. It is meant for someone who prefers a file they can edit and understand over a new subscription app.

This preview does not claim a personal founder story, customer testimonials, or proven business results. The SitKit name is a working name and its domain and trademark availability have not been checked.

What the workbook can and cannot do

It can help you keep a booking log, calculate amounts from inputs, and review a month of records. It does not communicate with clients, run a schedule, handle money, protect sensitive records, or replace professional bookkeeping.

Built for written communication

The planned support channel is email. The public support address, payment provider, digital delivery, and business details still need to be set up before the store accepts an order. There is no phone-sales process planned.

Store status: no checkout is connected, and no product orders are open. This page is a concept preview.
