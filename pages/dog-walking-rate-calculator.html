How this calculator works

It estimates monthly billable service hours from your weekly hands-on hours and the percentage of time you expect to bill. It divides that time by the typical visit length, then spreads your pay target and monthly expenses across the estimated visits.

The result is an average starting figure. It does not decide rates for overnight stays, holidays, extra pets, travel distances, cancellations, or services with different durations. Estimate those separately using your own costs and policies.

Build a price you can explain

Before setting a public rate, list the exact service and what it includes. Decide how you price extra pets, holidays, late changes, and travel. Check local prices, then review whether the work fits your schedule and costs.
