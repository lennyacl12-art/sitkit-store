DIGITAL PRODUCT PREVIEW

Digital delivery and refund information.

The current tracker concept is a downloadable spreadsheet. The store is not taking orders, so a final delivery or refund policy is not active yet.

Product format planned

The SitKit business tracker is prepared as an Excel .xlsx workbook. The product page describes the tabs and intended use. There is no physical item, inventory, or shipping route.

Downloads are not available through this preview

A secure checkout and digital file-delivery service have not been connected. This website does not deliver a product after payment and does not accept payment. Do not submit an order through this preview.

Refund and cancellation terms

No sale is currently open, so no purchase or refund terms are in force. Before launch, the business owner must choose and publish a clear policy that fits the payment provider, product, and applicable consumer rules. The final policy should explain how a customer can contact support and what happens if a download fails.

Before a store opens

  • Connect a secure payment provider and test a real delivery flow.
  • Publish the seller's legal name, contact details, price, and product scope.
  • Explain download format, compatibility, and support.
  • Publish applicable cancellation, refund, privacy, and sales-tax details.
Current status: concept only. No orders, charges, refunds, or digital deliveries can be made on this site.
