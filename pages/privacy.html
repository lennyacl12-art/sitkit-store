PRE-LAUNCH PRIVACY NOTES

Privacy information for this preview.

This page describes the current prototype only. The final seller, hosting, analytics, payment, and support providers have not been selected or connected.

Information entered on this site

The current preview has no contact form, checkout, account creation, or mailing-list sign-up. The rate calculator runs in the visitor's browser and does not submit its entered numbers to a server through the page code.

Hosting and external services

Once hosted publicly, the hosting provider may process standard technical data such as request logs. The Translate button opens Google Translate in a separate tab and attempts to copy the current page address so a visitor can paste it into that service. Google handles data under its own terms and privacy information.

Business records in the workbook

The downloadable workbook concept is a standard spreadsheet file. It is not encrypted or a secure client database. Do not store passwords, home-access codes, payment-card details, or sensitive medical information in it. Keep personal and pet information only in systems suitable for your purpose.

What must be added before launch

The future operator must replace this draft with accurate details for the actual host, payment provider, delivery service, email inbox, analytics or cookies, retention practices, and the method customers can use to ask privacy questions or exercise applicable rights.

Draft status: this is informational pre-launch text, not a final privacy policy or legal advice.

Questions cannot be sent through this preview because no support inbox is connected. See the contact page for the current status.
