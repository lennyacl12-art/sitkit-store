PRODUCT FAQ

Questions about the pet sitter tracker.

The product and storefront are still in pre-launch preview. Here is what the current workbook concept does and what is not set up yet.

THE SHORT ANSWERS

Clear details
before launch.

No phone call is required to review the product. Written support is planned for launch.

Can I order the tracker right now?

No. The page is a concept preview. The checkout and secure digital delivery service are not connected, and no orders are being accepted.

What is the planned product?

An editable .xlsx workbook for independent pet sitters and dog walkers. The workbook has a booking log, monthly dashboard, client and pet records, service list, expense log, and rate-planning calculator.

Will it work in Excel and Google Sheets?

It is built as an Excel workbook. The Start Here sheet explains how to import it into Google Sheets. Formatting may differ after import, so review formulas and layout in your chosen app before relying on it.

Is it an app or scheduling service?

No. It does not sync with a calendar, send reminders, manage staff, message clients, collect payments, or create a client portal.

Does it include a contract or tax forms?

No. It is a record-keeping and planning spreadsheet. It does not provide legal documents, accounting services, tax calculations, or financial advice.

Will I receive a physical item?

No. The product concept is a digital workbook. There is no physical inventory or shipping.

How will downloads work?

A secure file-delivery service must be connected before launch. The pre-launch store does not deliver a paid file yet.

Can I use the website in another language?

The language menu opens Google's website translation flow and copies the current page URL. Machine translation may be imperfect. This does not create separate translated pages for Google Search.

How can I get support?

Support is planned by email. A real support address and working inbox will be added before orders open. There is no phone-sales process planned.

What is the refund policy?

No purchases are open, so no refund policy is currently in force. A clear policy will be published before checkout launches.
